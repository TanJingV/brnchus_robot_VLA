# Figure caption

**English.** MuJoCo modelling of the two-segment tendon-driven continuum robot. **a,** Overall architecture of the 54-mm continuum assembly, comprising two 21-mm active segments, a 6-mm inter-section rigid connector and a 6-mm distal rigid tip; the base is excluded from the stated length. Tendons W1–W3 terminate at the inter-section connector, whereas W4–W6 traverse both active segments. **b,** Each active segment is discretized into 12 serial elements (element length, 1.75 mm), with paired orthogonal hinge joints representing biaxial bending. Joint stiffness is assigned according to beam theory, and tendon forces are applied through MuJoCo spatial-tendon paths. **c,** Cross-sectional tendon layout. Six 0.15-mm-diameter tendons are positioned at a radius of 1.54 mm around a 2.6-mm working channel within the 3.5-mm outer diameter and alternate at 60° intervals.

**中文。** 双段腱驱连续体机器人的MuJoCo建模示意图。**a，** 总长54 mm的连续体组件由两个21 mm主动段、一个6 mm段间刚性连接和一个6 mm远端刚性头组成，所标长度不含基座；W1–W3终止于段间连接，W4–W6贯穿两个主动段。**b，** 每个主动段离散为12个串联单元（单元长度1.75 mm），每个离散站采用两个正交转动关节描述双向弯曲，并根据梁理论设置关节刚度，通过MuJoCo空间腱路径施加驱动力。**c，** 截面腱线排布：六根直径0.15 mm的驱动丝以1.54 mm半径围绕直径2.6 mm的工作通道交替排列，相邻驱动丝间隔60°，连续体外径为3.5 mm。
